Acme Corp International Travel Policy: Employees traveling internationally for business must submit a travel request through the Acme Travel Portal at least 14 business days before departure. All flights must be booked in economy class for trips under 6 hours and premium economy for trips exceeding 6 hours, unless a director-level exception is approved. Employees must carry a valid passport with at least 6 months of remaining validity and any required visas before departure. A pre-travel briefing with the Global Security team is mandatory for trips to countries on the elevated-risk list.

Acme Corp Domestic Travel Policy: Domestic business travel within the country does not require director approval if the estimated cost is under $1,500. Employees should use the preferred airline and hotel partners listed on the Travel Portal to take advantage of negotiated corporate rates. Rental cars are permitted only for trips where public transportation or ride-hailing services are unavailable or impractical.

Acme Corp Expense Reimbursement Policy: All business-related expenses, including international and domestic travel, must be submitted through the Expense Management System within 30 days of the trip's completion. Original receipts are required for any single expense over $25. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to a daily per-diem of $75 for domestic trips and $100 for international trips. Alcohol purchases are not reimbursable under any circumstance.

Acme Corp Remote Work Policy: Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require Vice President sign-off and are reviewed quarterly. Remote employees are responsible for maintaining a secure home office setup, including a locked device screen when away from the workstation and use of the company VPN for all corporate systems access.

Acme Corp Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy: Full-time employees accrue 15 days of PTO per year during their first two years of employment, increasing to 20 days after the second anniversary and 25 days after the fifth anniversary. PTO requests should be submitted at least 5 business days in advance through the HR Portal. Unused PTO up to 5 days may be carried over into the next calendar year; any remaining balance is forfeited.

Acme Corp Code of Conduct: All employees are expected to act with integrity, honesty, and respect toward colleagues, clients, and partners. Conflicts of interest, including outside employment or financial interests that could influence business decisions, must be disclosed to the Ethics Office. Retaliation against employees who report concerns in good faith is strictly prohibited and subject to disciplinary action up to and including termination.

Acme Corp IT Security Policy: All company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and be updated with the latest security patches within 7 days of release. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for access to all corporate applications, including email, the Expense Management System, and the Travel Portal. Employees must report any suspected phishing email or security incident to the IT Security team within 24 hours of discovery.

Acme Corp Product Overview - Acme Cloud Suite: Acme Cloud Suite is a business productivity platform offering document collaboration, video conferencing, and secure file storage. It supports single sign-on (SSO) integration with major identity providers and offers a 99.9% uptime service level agreement (SLA) for enterprise customers. The platform is available on a per-seat monthly subscription with volume discounts for organizations over 500 seats.

Acme Corp Customer Support Policy: Standard support tickets submitted by enterprise customers receive an initial response within 4 business hours, while critical severity incidents are addressed within 30 minutes, 24/7. Support is available via phone, email, and live chat. Customers on the Premium Support tier are assigned a dedicated Customer Success Manager who conducts quarterly business reviews.
